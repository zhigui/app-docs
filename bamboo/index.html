Bamboo

AI Markdown Notes for writing, tasks, and personal knowledge management.

Download on the App Store open in new window Read the Docs

Markdown-first Writing

Capture ideas, write long-form notes, manage tasks, and keep images or attachments together in clean, portable Markdown.

Connected Knowledge

Organize notes with nested Collections, link ideas with Wiki Links, and quickly find important content with Pin, Star, Archive, and search.

AI That Fits Your Workflow

Configure your own AI provider and model, then use AI to summarize, rewrite, draft, brainstorm, and work with your notes.

Capture and Move Freely

Clip web pages into Markdown, import existing Markdown libraries, export to portable formats, and create full backups when needed.

Across Apple Devices

Use Bamboo on iPhone, iPad, and Mac, with iCloud Sync, widgets, App Links, and Shortcuts-friendly workflows.

Power User Ready

On Mac, Bamboo supports bamboocli and MCP server workflows for developers, researchers, and advanced automation.
